our store front operates through email order. click through the catalogue in your own time. it might be smart to note down the item code(s) of the thing(s) you wish to purchase.

download the order form and fill out your details. clicking the last button will open an email adressed to us. if you are so inclined, attach your completed form and send it off. happy shopping

  • please read the instructions on the order form thoroughly before submitting.
  • ensure your form is saved as a 'pdf' or 'word' document so our servers can read it correctly.
  • you will recieve an invoice via email when your order has been processed. we ship orders out every tuesday; you will be notified when your order has been shipped.
  • none of the information you provide in the form is kept after your order is fulfilled.
  • if you have any inquiries (including grievances) regarding your order, please write us an email at the same address detailing your situation and we will try for a timely response.
    • if doing so, please change the subject line to 'inquiry' so the email is sorted correctly.
